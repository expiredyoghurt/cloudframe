Weather & clock







Official NEA data via data.gov.sg (Singapore only). PSI and UV show in daylight hours only (not during sleep hours); PSI refreshes hourly, the nowcast and UV every 30 minutes.

Auto brightness


Only works on devices and browsers that expose an ambient light sensor (some Android/Chrome devices, often behind a browser flag). iPads, iPhones and most laptops don't. It dims the photos in software; it can't change the hardware backlight. The frame's control bar shows what it found.

Sleep mode




Photos dim, and the time, day, date, weather, temperature and tomorrow's forecast show large in the middle (if the overlay is on). Uses the timezone set under Weather & clock.
